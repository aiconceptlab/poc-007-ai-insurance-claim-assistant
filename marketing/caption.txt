POC #007: AI Insurance Claim Assistant 💻

A damaged laptop. A clearer claim.

Upload a photo. Explain what happened. Get an editable claim draft for a person to review.

This POC brings together:
→ Tentative observations from a damage photo
→ A spoken or typed incident account
→ Structured fields you can check and edit
→ A checklist that flags missing evidence
→ A downloadable draft with supporting photos

In the sample: a cracked screen, a damaged casing, and one missing purchase receipt.

The draft shows 92% checklist completeness. That number measures the presence of the demo's fields and attachments—not the chance of approval.

AI prepares the draft. A person reviews the claim.
Nothing is approved or submitted automatically.

Built with vision, speech-to-text and structured output, plus a small Node.js backend. No insurer integration needed for the POC.

The source code and no-key sample are free. Live AI uses your own API credits. Carousel examples are illustrative.
